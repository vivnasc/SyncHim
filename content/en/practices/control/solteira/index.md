# PRACTICES, THE CONTROL (single)

5 practices, one between each pair of sessions.

---

## PRACTICE 1, After Session 2

**Notice the Control.** 3 days.

For 3 days, **observe** each time you control, evaluate, test, or eliminate. People, situations, possibilities.

Change nothing. Just see.

Each night, write: *Today I controlled/evaluated/tested _____ times. In _____ of them, I saw and didn't act.*

---

## PRACTICE 2, After Session 3

**Don't Eliminate on First Impression.** 3 days.

Don't disqualify anyone over the first flaw. When the impulse appears, ask: *is this real incompatibility, or is it my control looking for an exit?*

Distinguish a real red flag (disrespect, lies) from a control excuse (spelling error, dressed badly).

Each night, write: *Today I wanted to eliminate _____ times. _____ were control, not incompatibility.*

---

## PRACTICE 3, After Session 4

**Drop One Defence a Day.** 3 days.

Each day, drop a small act of control. Let someone decide. Share something true before being certain. Don't test.

Each night, write: *Today I let go of control in _____. I felt _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 4, After Session 5

**Let It Happen.** 3 days.

Each day, let one situation happen without managing the outcome. Stay with the uncertainty without taking back control.

Each night, write: *Today I let _____ happen. I felt _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 5, After Session 6

**Trust Without Guaranteeing.** 3 days (and forever).

Whenever you notice you were about to control, stop. Breathe once. Ask yourself:

> *"What if I trusted, right now, without needing to guarantee the outcome?"*

You don't have to trust more. Just ask the question. It releases the control.

Each night, write: *Today, when I asked if I could trust without guaranteeing, _____ appeared.*

This practice doesn't end. The other 4 were preparation. This one stays.
