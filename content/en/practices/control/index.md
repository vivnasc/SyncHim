# PRACTICES, THE CONTROL (EN)

5 practices, one between each pair of sessions.

---

## PRACTICE 1, After Session 2

**Notice.** 3 days.

For 3 days, just **notice** without changing anything. Each time you feel the impulse to correct, anticipate, or organize his life, stop. Don't act. Watch the impulse as if it belonged to someone else. Notice what you'd feel if you didn't act.

You don't have to not act. You just have to **see** that you're acting.

Each night, write: *Today I noticed _____ times I was about to correct/anticipate/organize. In _____ of them, I acted anyway. In _____ of them, I saw and didn't act.*

---

## PRACTICE 2, After Session 3

**Don't Correct.** 3 days.

Choose **one specific thing** your husband does "wrong." For 3 days, **don't correct**. Not with words. Not with tone. Not with a look. Not by redoing it after.

If you feel the inner itch, stay with the itch. Don't act on it.

Each night, write: *Today, when he _____, I didn't correct. What I felt was _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 3, After Session 4

**One Decision a Day That Isn't Yours.** 3 days.

Each day, leave one small decision to him. Without giving your opinion before. Without correcting during. Without commenting after.

If he asks your opinion: *"I trust you, you decide."* Nothing more.

Each night, write: *Today I let him decide _____. What I felt was _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 4, After Session 5

**Let It Drop.** 3 days.

Each day, let one small thing drop that you'd normally have managed. See what happens without intervening.

Don't collect it back. Don't compensate after.

Each night, write: *Today I let _____ drop. What happened was _____. What I felt was _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 5, After Session 6

**Trust Before Seeing.** 3 days (and the rest of life).

Whenever you notice you're anticipating a failure of his, stop. Breathe once. Ask yourself:

> *"What if I trusted, without needing prior proof that I can?"*

You don't have to trust more. Just ask the question. The question releases the system that was on watch.

Each night, write: *Today, when I asked if I could trust without proof, _____ appeared.*

This practice doesn't end. The other 4 were preparation. This one stays.
