# 2. PRIVACY POLICY

**Route:** `/en/privacy`
**Last updated:** May 16, 2026

## Principle

We collect the minimum necessary. We don't sell data. We don't share with third parties beyond what's strictly necessary for the product to function.

## What we collect

**To create your account:**
- Email
- Name (optional)
- Preferred language

**For the product to function:**
- Your diagnostic answers
- Your written responses in sessions 3-7
- Diagnostic history (dates and dominant knots)
- Session progress

**For technical analysis:**
- Anonymous usage events (session started, session completed, etc.)
- IP (for region detection and fraud prevention)
- Device type

**For payment:**
- Payment data processed by Stripe (we don't have access to your card)
- Stripe customer ID

## What we do NOT collect

- We don't have access to your credit card
- We don't use third-party tracking cookies (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel) without your explicit consent
- We don't sell your data to anyone
- We don't train AI models with your written responses

## Where your data lives

- **Account and responses:** Supabase (EU servers)
- **Payment:** Stripe (per Stripe's policy)
- **Transactional email:** Resend (per Resend's policy)
- **Web infrastructure:** Cloudflare (CDN, no personal data storage)

## Encryption

Your written responses in sessions — which may contain sensitive information about your childhood, relationship, and family — are encrypted at rest in Supabase.

## Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

1. **Access** your data — at `/account` you can see everything we have about you.
2. **Correct** incorrect data.
3. **Delete** your account and all associated data. Button at `/account/delete`. Full deletion within 30 days.
4. **Export** your data in JSON format. Button at `/account/export`.
5. **Withdraw consent** at any time.

For any data-related request, write to **privacy@syncehim.com**. We respond within 10 business days.

## Cookies

We use a single essential technical cookie: `syncehim_locale` (saves your language preference). We don't use tracking cookies.

## Minors

SyncHim is for adult women. We do not intentionally collect data from anyone under 18. If we discover we have collected data from a minor, we delete it immediately.

Because after Session 3, you'll have seen material that only makes sense if you complete the crossing. Refunding after Session 3 would be paying you to read the method. That's not the deal.
