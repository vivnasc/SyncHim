# Before we begin

You've arrived.

You walked through the door. You read what was on the other side. You decided, even with hesitation, that it was worth trying.

Now we're here, you and I, on this screen no one else sees.

I'm going to ask you something before the diagnostic opens.

**Sit in silence for a moment.**

Put the phone down. Breathe. Sit the way you sit when no one is watching.

And ask yourself, with the honesty only you will know:

> *How long have I been living like this?*

Not the fight. Not the crisis. **This.** This silent way of being close to someone and alone at the same time.

Maybe a number appears, months, years. Maybe a specific scene, a night, a dinner, the day you realised something had shifted and no one said it. Maybe nothing appears, only **tiredness**.

All of it counts.

Don't answer to anyone. Just notice.

---

Before we move on, three things you need to know.

**First:** the diagnostic isn't a personality test. It won't tell you whether you're "sensitive feminine" or "wild feminine" or any other magazine category. It will tell you **the name of the old pattern in you** that is pushing your husband away without you seeing. Every woman has hers. There are seven. Yours will appear clearly at the end.

**Second:** none of what you answer will be seen by anyone. Not by me. Not by your husband. By no one. Your answers stay only with you, in a space only you access. You can retake the diagnostic tomorrow, in a month, in a year, as many times as you want, free, forever.

**Third:** if midway through you recognise this is too close, you can stop and return. Your answers stay saved. There's no rush. This process is yours, and the time is yours.

---

One question before the diagnostic opens.

**Are you willing, for 8 minutes, to look at what you've been avoiding for months?**
