## VISIBLE TITLE

Before we begin.

## FULL TEXT

You're not here by accident.

It was probably night when you clicked. Your husband was probably next to you, asleep, or on his phone, or in another room — and you, even in the same house, were alone.

It's not the first time.

How long have you felt this?

Not the fight. Not the crisis. **This.** The silent thing, without a name, that has lived between you for months or years. The small gestures that no longer happen — the kiss he no longer gives you when leaving, the joke he no longer tells, the hand that no longer reaches for yours in bed.

From the outside, everything looks fine. No one notices. The children don't notice. Your mother doesn't notice. Your friends compliment you both. You still have dinner together, still go on holiday, still have sex sometimes — though less, and differently.

But inside, you know.

You know he no longer looks for you the way he used to. That he looks at his phone more than at you. That he answers "everything's fine" when you ask, and you know it isn't, but you no longer insist because insisting is tiring.

You know you've changed too. That you complain more. Or that you complain less, but inside you keep a list. That you wait for him to understand without you saying — and when he doesn't, you punish in silence.

You know you've tried to talk. Tried the romantic getaway. Read articles. Considered couples therapy. None of it touched what's actually happening.

I know what this is. I lived exactly this.

**Your marriage isn't dying from lack of love. It's dying because you've come out of sync.**

Couples function through sync. It's not a metaphor — it's how two bodies in proximity operate over time. When one falls out of phase, the other feels it, even without naming it. A silent distance forms, and that distance grows on its own if it isn't seen.

What you've tried failed because it treated the symptom — his coldness, the silence, the lack of touch. The cause is somewhere else. It's in an old pattern of yours — not a flaw, an old scar still operating as it learned to operate — that's pushing the sync out without you seeing.

Over the next 21 days, we'll find that pattern in you, and we'll return to sync. I won't teach you tricks. I won't give you scripts to send. I won't ask you to do anything *with him*. I'll ask you to do something just with yourself — because the sync that lasts always begins in the woman.

Every three days, a session opens. Between one and the next, you'll have a practice. Not a task. Something you live in silence. You don't need to say anything to your husband. You don't need to visibly change anything.

In 21 days, you'll come back to this session and read these words again. You'll notice something has shifted.

Before we move on, I need you to answer one question. Honestly. No one is judging — your answer is saved only for you.
