## The guarantee

7-day unconditional guarantee.

If, after reading Session 1, you don't recognise yourself in what's written, or if, for any reason, this process isn't what you expected, you ask for a refund and we return the full amount.

No questions. No friction. No need to justify.

## How to request

1. Write to **refund@syncehim.com** within 7 days of purchase.
2. Include the email associated with your account.
3. Within 5 business days, we process the full refund via PayPal.
4. The amount returns to your card in 5-10 business days (depending on your bank).

## What we refund

- Tier 1 (US$ 39), in full.
- Tier 2 (US$ 87), in full.
- Additional knot upgrade (US$ 19), in full.

## What you lose

Access to paid content. You retain access to the free Tier 0 (Sessions 1 and 2).

## After 7 days

After 7 days from purchase, there's no refund. We're available to speak with you if there's a technical problem or something has gone wrong, but financial refund is only available in the first 7 days.
