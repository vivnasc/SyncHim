# PRACTICES, THE HUNGER (EN)

5 practices, one between each pair of sessions.

---

## PRACTICE 1, After Session 2

**Don't Initiate.** 3 days.

For 3 days, don't initiate conversation with him first. Not a message. Not a call. Not a question at dinner. Not a comment in the kitchen.

Not punitive silence. Not a trick. Observation.

You'll notice the urge to initiate appears 20, 30, 50 times across these 3 days. Each time, notice what you were feeling before wanting to speak.

Rules:
- If he initiates, reply normally.
- Logistics and urgencies (kids, household), communicate.
- If he asks if you're okay, say "yes."
- Each night, write: *How many times today did I want to initiate? What was I feeling before?*

---

## PRACTICE 2, After Session 3

**Don't Answer the Hunger.** 3 days.

When you feel hunger for him, urge to message, ask where he is, check his phone, don't answer the hunger. Ask:

> *"What would I feel right now if he didn't exist?"*

Stay with the question 5 minutes. Don't search for an answer. Just stay.

Rules:
- Not to imagine life without him. To look at yourself, this moment, without him as reference.
- Relief might appear. Panic might appear. Nothing might appear. All counts.
- Each night, one line: *Today I felt hunger _____ times. When I asked what I'd feel without him, _____ appeared.*

---

## PRACTICE 3, After Session 4

**Five Minutes of You.** 3 days.

Reserve 5 minutes per day to ask: *what am I feeling right now?*

Sit. Phone out of reach. Eyes closed. Stay.

Rules:
- If mind wanders to him, return.
- Nothing might come. That's information.
- Tiredness, small anger, empty sadness, boredom, a wish, stay with whatever appears.
- Each night, one line: *Today, during the 5 minutes, I felt _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 4, After Session 5

**Arrive at Yourself First.** 3 days.

Whenever he arrives home, don't turn to him immediately. Continue what you were doing.

Difference: 10 seconds. No more.

Rules:
- Without hostility. Without performed indifference. With presence in yourself.
- Urge to stand and go to him will come. Sit back. Finish your gesture.
- Each night, one line: *Today, when he arrived, I _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 5, After Session 6

**Return.** 3 days (and the rest of life).

Whenever you notice the knot acting, stop. Breathe once. Ask:

> *"What do I, in this moment, outside of him, want?"*

Rules:
- You don't have to do it. You don't have to change anything external.
- Just ask. Notice what appears.
- Any answer counts. Including "I don't know."
- Each night, one line: *Today, when I asked what I wanted outside of him, _____ appeared.*

This practice doesn't end. The other four were preparation. This one stays.
