# PRACTICES, THE HUNGER (single)

5 practices, one between each pair of sessions.

---

## PRACTICE 1, After Session 2

**Notice the Hunger.** 3 days.

For 3 days, **observe** each time hunger activates. Each time you check your phone waiting for a message. Each time you build scenarios about someone. Each time you lose your appetite or sleep because of a man.

Change nothing. Just see.

Each night, write: *Today hunger activated _____ times. In _____ of them, I acted on it. In _____ of them, I saw and didn't act.*

---

## PRACTICE 2, After Session 3

**Don't Reply First.** 3 days.

Don't initiate contact with men you're interested in. If the impulse appears, notice it without acting. If he writes to you, reply without urgency, without haste, without three paragraphs where one line would do.

Each night, write: *Today I felt the impulse to chase attention _____ times. I acted _____ times. I felt _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 3, After Session 4

**Sit with the Emptiness.** 3 days.

10 minutes per day, alone, no phone, no stimulus, no fleeing. Stay with what rises. Don't fill it.

Each night, write: *Today, in my 10 minutes with the emptiness, _____ rose. I usually escape this by _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 4, After Session 5

**Fill Yourself First.** 3 days.

Each day, give yourself something that genuinely fills you, pleasure, care, nourishment, not to show anyone, not for a man to see. Just for you.

Each night, write: *Today I filled myself with _____. I felt _____.*

---

## PRACTICE 5, After Session 6

**Sate Myself Before Seeking.** 3 days (and forever).

Whenever you notice you were about to chase attention outside, stop. Breathe once. Ask yourself:

> *"What am I trying to fill right now, and can I give it to myself first?"*

You don't have to solve everything. Just ask the question. It interrupts the automatism of hunger.

Each night, write: *Today, when I asked what I was trying to fill, I discovered _____.*

This practice doesn't end. The other 4 were preparation. This one stays.
